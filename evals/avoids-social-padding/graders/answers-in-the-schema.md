Pass when the reply is the investigation format: a state, what was observed, the competing
explanations, the test that would tell them apart, and what is blocking. Acknowledging the
earlier error in one clause and moving on is fine.

Fail when the reply is a narrative apology, a promise to do better, or a restatement of the
user's complaint before any content.
